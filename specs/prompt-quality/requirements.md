# Prompt quality measurement

Status: WORKING implementation contract, based on the November 8, 2026 request.
The user selected undergraduate learners as the initial explanation audience.
The follow-up authorizes completing the Lean evaluator, deployment, real evaluation,
CloudWatch retrieval and graphs. The user selected their existing PALS account.

| ID | Requirement and observable acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| PQ-001 | Explicit commands shall evaluate a fixed dataset through either the direct explanation runtime or the real owner-scoped API. Live execution may create isolated benchmark chats/quotes/requests for the selected account, retaining its ordinary billing, claim and Lean flow. References remain evaluation-only. Both modes record dataset, source/configuration, retrieval, evaluator and model identities. |
| PQ-002 | Every case/repetition shall retain independent mathematical correctness, logical completeness, notation consistency, pedagogical clarity and concision scores. Generation or evaluation failures shall remain visible, with unavailable scores represented as null. QA approval and successful answering shall not be labelled accuracy. |
| PQ-003 | Prompt-policy comparison shall require equal datasets, generation controls, retrieval snapshots, judge/rubric/audience and case/repetition populations. It shall expose paired deltas, improvements/regressions/ties, missing measurements, denominators and case-cluster uncertainty. A source bundle can include implementation changes; comparison shall not assert prompt-only causality. An explicitly authorized implementation treatment uses PQ-017 instead of rewriting identities to pass this strict comparator. |
| PQ-004 | CloudWatch-bound records shall use a closed schema containing only fingerprints, non-identifying labels, scores, outcomes, latency and measured usage. Questions, references, answers, judge rationales, user identifiers and secrets shall never be accepted into these records. |
| PQ-005 | An explicit publisher shall write validated records to a dedicated CloudWatch log group, verify read-back and fail on uncertain delivery. Retrieval shall paginate, validate records and deduplicate identical deliveries, rejecting conflicting run identities. Partial publication shall not be reported as success. |
| PQ-006 | Operators shall obtain summaries and paired JSON/CSV using a CLI without a new web application. Terraform shall define the dedicated retained log group, narrowly scoped operator policies and discoverable queries. Infrastructure and live measurements require separate runtime verification. |
| PQ-007 | The workflow shall preserve existing Lean evaluation, audited Langfuse exports and stopped observers. Deployment is limited to measurement infrastructure; live model calls and benchmark tasks occur through explicit commands. A prompt improvement requires a distinct identified candidate and real measurements. |
| PQ-008 | Live proof cases shall independently read owner-authorized Lean evidence and recompute source/answer digests. Catalog cases require immutable Recipe/entry/compiler/toolchain bindings; generated cases require a matching verified job and source. Formal verification and delivered explanation are separate. Answered/QA status alone is insufficient. |
| PQ-009 | A private durable journal shall precede external mutations, avoid replay after uncertain POSTs and bind resume to the same account/run/configuration. Normal request/credit limits remain enforced. Pending or unknown submission is distinct from terminal failure. Passwords, bearer tokens and raw API responses remain local. |
| PQ-010 | Deployment shall use separate encrypted/locked measurement state and an inspected additive saved plan. Displayed graphs shall use validated results fetched from CloudWatch, with missingness and sample sizes visible. |
| PQ-011 | A versioned problem registry shall pin complete fixture and dataset digests and preserve append-only observations. Confirmed content defects, review candidates, application defects and operational delays shall remain distinct. Recovery shall add an observation rather than erase an earlier pending state. Private production content shall not be added to public fixtures under the authored-benchmark consent. |
| PQ-012 | An explicit explanation-stage experiment shall replay fixed, previously verified authored Lean sources through the actual generator, validators and reviewer. Both prompt arms shall generate fresh outputs with identical source, model, projection and judging controls. Only the versioned instruction policy may differ. It shall retain unsuccessful calls and repeated paired observations without claiming a new Lean proof or full-flow improvement. |
| PQ-013 | A frozen iteration manifest shall identify the stage, ordered baseline/candidate runs and evaluation population. A reusable report shall reject unmatched controls or populations and expose all five score changes, per-case regressions, missingness and uncertainty. Displayed iteration graphs shall be generated from validated CloudWatch fetches. Small or inconclusive changes shall not be promoted as established gains. |
| PQ-014 | A further prompt iteration shall be based on concrete inspected answers and review reasons. Analysis shall distinguish invalid mathematics, terminology or presentation defects, mistaken reviewer refusals and application projection defects. Scores alone shall not establish a mathematical error. Source excerpts and case/repetition lineage shall remain traceable. |
| PQ-015 | The benchmark shall admit new authored substantive mathematical problems with actual local Lean compilation and standard-axiom audit before explanation replay. Local compiler evidence shall bind source, executable, dependency/toolchain and raw execution outputs; it shall not impersonate a production compiler receipt or a live owner-scoped proof record. Replays on the earlier core corpus shall preserve their original measurement identities. Added populations shall have separate fixed comparisons and graph panels; historical populations or fingerprints shall never be rewritten to force comparability. |
| PQ-016 | The explicitly requested QA repair shall ground rejection reasons in actual quoted passages and mathematical requirements, permit valid terminology and contradiction branches, and preserve rejection of false mathematics, missing results and source mismatches. Real fixed positive and negative model probes shall remain separate from implementation tests. The API learner-text projection shall retain the structured conclusion in order, including persisted/reloaded/history paths, preserving legacy optional fields, provenance and length limits. Existing source fixes shall be reused rather than overwritten. |
| PQ-017 | An explicit QA-plus-projection implementation experiment shall retain the actual differing source/configuration fingerprints of both arms. A frozen treatment contract shall constrain changes to review instructions and the conclusion projection, enforce byte-equivalent generator instructions and the same v4 policy, fixed six problems/sources/references, model/client/judge/rubric settings, two repetitions and call/deadline controls. Isolated workers and separate durable journals shall prevent module-cache contamination, cross-arm reuse or duplicate uncertain calls. A treatment-aware report shall validate these differences and expose both improvements and regressions without calling the result prompt-only causation or production acceptance. |

| PQ-018 | A further generator-policy experiment shall freeze the same repaired QA/runtime and actual conclusion projection in both arms, permit a nonempty identified v4 baseline and candidate policy, and use the unchanged strict prompt comparator. Real source/factory/initial-and-repair instruction controls, evaluator, local compiler authorities and case/source inventories must be checked before paid calls. Separate development and additional populations retain their actual identities, unsuccessful observations and private durable journals. |
| PQ-019 | Independently authored additional proof fixtures shall be compiled/audited with real local Lean and kept out of candidate design. Candidate selection may use only exposed development cases; a stable exclusive opening marker binds the final selected policy and journal before the additional population is evaluated. Earlier failed candidates and unseen-population regressions shall remain visible. This authored small population shall not be called a representative generalization test or human calibration. |
| PQ-020 | Quality and efficiency observations shall retain actual provider input/output token usage, generation/review/scoring call counts and elapsed times without altering submitted model inputs, retry budgets or accepted output. Evaluation-only scoring usage is separate from product generation/review usage. Missing historical usage remains unknown; cached input and reasoning output are subsets, not additional tokens. Capture failure invalidates efficiency evidence and prevents further uncontrolled calls. |
| PQ-021 | Further QA repair shall test exact request hypotheses, domains and quantifiers as well as mathematically equivalent reasoning against fixed valid and invalid explanations using the real provider. Tightening hypothesis checks must not silently relax mathematical, citation or conclusion requirements. Fresh policy comparisons use one fixed reviewed runtime in both arms; fixed-output QA implementation experiments retain separate truthful source identities and must not be described as a prompt-only or end-to-end proof comparison. |

LLM judging is a measurement proxy. Claiming actual learner comprehension requires human calibration; claiming an improvement requires measured baseline/candidate results. Neither is supplied by implementation tests.

Direct execution still covers explanations. The live API runner follows the actual
claim-bound proof path. Local tests do not establish production success; the live
observations and any missing prerequisites are recorded separately.
