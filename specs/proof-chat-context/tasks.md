## Completion and execution limits

The current deliverable is the three WORKING spec files with complete ID coverage. Implementation completion requires PCX-T01–PCX-T08 evidence; live issue closure additionally requires PCX-T09–PCX-T10 under separately scoped release authority. Unavailable PostgreSQL, signing, provider or verifier checks remain NOT VERIFIED; do not replace them with a bypass or weaken a failing assertion.

Each delegated slice must have disjoint write ownership or read-only review scope, pinned revisions, explicit verification and a bounded stage budget. Commands are bounded to 60 seconds, with at most two transient retries and two no-progress cycles before returning preserved evidence. The parent owns cancellation and scope/commit/release decisions. No duplicated app/service, model-selector work or production operation belongs to this working-spec task.
