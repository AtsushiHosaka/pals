# Constant-curl boundary circulation supplement

This append-only one-card supplement uses the frozen `typed-vector-calculus-v6` profile. The original five-card manifest, Lean bundle, validator, and registry remain byte-identical. Its proposed binding is `univ.multivar.line-surface-integrals / compute_construct / advanced`; parent classification review and actual matrix admission remain separate.

`vector_v6_constant_curl_circulation` proves that counterclockwise circulation of a general one-form `P dx + Q dy` around an ordered rectangle equals `c (b₁−a₁)(b₂−a₂)` when its scalar curl is identically c. It retains the full weak Green hypotheses: P and Q continuous on the closed rectangle, actual derivative fields A and B outside a countable interior exception set, integrable curl, and the equality `B(z)(1,0)−A(z)(0,1)=c` throughout the closed rectangle. This last equality is on the supplied derivative fields, which need not be actual derivatives at the permitted exceptional points. The explicit integrability premise is retained even though the constant-curl condition also makes it derivable on the finite rectangle.

The proof first applies the countable-exception Green identity, substitutes the constant integrand, identifies the rectangle as a product of real intervals, and computes product Lebesgue measure. Every boundary integral and its orientation remain explicit in Lean. This is a genuine boundary line-integral calculation for unknown general fields, not a restatement that a potential has path-independent integrals. The advanced classification reflects the combination of weak regularity, exceptional sets, Green's theorem, boundary orientation, and product-measure area. It does not assert surface integration or a general Stokes theorem.

The negative distinguishes scalar curl from divergence: the field `(-y,x)` has zero divergence and curl 2, so its circulation around every positive rectangle is twice the area. It is an authored negative candidate, not a measured retrieval result.
